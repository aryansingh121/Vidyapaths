# Vidyapaths — Design System

## Color Direction

### Primary
Deep Navy Blue
- Navbar, headings, primary cards, hero badges, footer
- Value: `#0B1F3A`

### Accent
Orange
- Primary CTA, highlights, badges, icons, active states
- Value: `#F97316` / `#EA580C`

### Background
White
- Clean, uncluttered reading surfaces
- Value: `#FFFFFF`

### Supporting Neutrals
- Slate grays (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`, `#64748B`, `#334155`)

## Typography
- Headings: Poppins (Bold / Semibold)
- Body: Inter (Regular / Medium)

## UI Style
- Rounded cards (`rounded-2xl`, `rounded-3xl`)
- Soft shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-xl`)
- Crisp thin borders (`border-slate-200/80`)
- Generous whitespace
- Strong typographic hierarchy
- Subtle hover animations & transitions

## Buttons
- Primary: Orange background + White text (`bg-orange-500 hover:bg-orange-600`)
- Secondary: Navy (`bg-navy-900`) or White outline (`border border-slate-200`)
- Action-oriented copy: "Get Free Counselling", "Explore Colleges", "Apply Now"
